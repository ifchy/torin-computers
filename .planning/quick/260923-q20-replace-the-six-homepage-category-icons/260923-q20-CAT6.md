# Category 6 is an exhibit, not a lead source

**Owner ruling, 2026-09-23:** category 6 will not win customers. Companies running medical or
industrial equipment do not bring it to a shop this size, and it was never realistic that they
would. Its purpose is to make the shop read as more competent and trustworthy to the
**cat-1..cat-5** customer — the person with a cracked screen or a liquid-damaged laptop.

## The dividend nobody was expecting: this dissolves #3c

`OWNER-QUESTIONS` #3c — *what is explicitly REFUSED* — is recorded as **"the expensive one and
the reason to hold"**, because the page "invites enquiries about medical and industrial equipment
and contains nothing that steers the wrong ones away… converts the page into unfiltered phone
traffic the shop has to triage by hand."

**That cost existed only because the page read as an invitation.** As an exhibit it largely
evaporates: the page is no longer soliciting hospital equipment, it is demonstrating board-level
competence to someone with a laptop. #3c is not answered — it is *de-escalated* from blocking to
worth-having, and a short refusal list would now serve discoverability rather than triage.

## What changed

|---|---|---|
| Card line | `[ASSUMED]` placeholder, customer voice, «…която другаде не приемат» | owner-written, shop voice, «ремонт на електронни платки за медицинска и индустриална апаратура» |

**Not one new claim was added.** Every clause already stood on the page or in an owner answer.
The reframing is ordering and purpose, which is exactly what `CONTEXT D3.5-08` permits and the
only thing it permits without new owner input.

## The card line, measured

The owner's first draft ran **80 characters** and rendered as **four lines** at both 360px and
390px, making cat-6 the tallest card by 28px. The shipped wording is **66** and holds three lines
at both, level with cards 1, 2 and 4.

**Character count does not predict this.** A different 66-character variant wrapped to four lines,
because where the words break matters more than how many there are. Re-measure, never estimate.

The voice shift from symptom-language to shop-language is **deliberate and recorded**: a symptom
line is the wrong instrument for a card whose job is to signal capability rather than match a
complaint.

## The refused proposal, and why it is recorded

The owner asked whether to advertise the high-end board-rework speciality — the shop **can** do it
but has no interest in taking the work, so the proposal was explicitly a ranking play. **Declined.**

1. **Wrong lever.** It matches queries for the jobs the shop does not want and barely touches the
   cat-1..cat-5 queries it does.
2. **It works against its own goal.** A service listed on the site and refused on the telephone
   produces exactly the negative review that damages AI recommendation — and third-party reviews
   dominate that channel far more than site copy does.
3. **Precedent already paid for.** Same shape as the discontinued-service claim D3.5-01 had to
   hunt off `remont-na-portove.html`, which cost a whole gap-closure plan.

**`truth-gate.js` settled it independently.** The first draft of the comment explaining the
refusal *named* the technique, and the gate failed the whole tree — it matches the token and
cannot read the sentence around it. Had the claim been added to served copy, the project's own
tooling would have hard-failed the build. The comment now describes rather than names, and says
why, so the next person to reconsider learns it from the file rather than from a red build.

The capability carrying the same credibility without the liability is **«ремонт на ниво
компонент»** — true, owner-confirmed, and now the first thing the page says.

## On AI discoverability, honestly

Site copy is necessary but **not** the main driver. Assistants recommending a local repair shop
lean hardest on third-party corroboration — Google Business Profile, reviews, directories. The
site mostly decides whether the shop *matches* a query and whether the claim is *trusted*, not
whether it surfaces at all.

What the site does control, and what was applied here: capability vocabulary over adjectives;
question-shaped content (the FAQ is the most machine-legible part of the page, which is why the
exclusion went there); and **cross-linking**, which is the actual mechanism — credibility parked
on a page nobody searches for earns nothing until it points at the pages that do.
