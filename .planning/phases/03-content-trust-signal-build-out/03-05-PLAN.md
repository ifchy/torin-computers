About page, and give the surge-damage page its real copy.

Two of these three carry a decision that is easy to get wrong in opposite directions. Category 6 has
no confirmed scope — OWNER-QUESTIONS #3 is launch-blocking — but `problem-stari.html` is an
already-indexed URL that will serve whatever is in the tree at cutover, so leaving it a placeholder

subject that URL already covers and to keep the *category promotion* gated: the record stays
unpublished, so the six-card grid and the navigation still route to the homepage anchor and nobody is
sold a headline service the shop has not described.

found the publicity obligation was scoped to an implementation period that ended in 2020, that
homepage placement was never required, and that the disclosure page reachable from a footer link
already exceeds the compliant minimum. So the homepage requirement is already satisfied structurally
and what remains is purely editorial — plus one thing that must be flagged rather than fixed: the
disclosure's results paragraph names a different company.

Output: three finished pages and one owner-facing flag.
