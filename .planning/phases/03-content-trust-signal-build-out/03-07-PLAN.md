Ship the remaining three category-2 child pages and open the publish gate on all five.

Two of the three carry an argument the site has never made explicitly, and both come from the shop's
own copy. The port pages explain that a broken USB jack can short supply rails straight into the
south bridge, which turns a connector problem into a board problem. The power-jack page carries the

can put supply voltage onto layers that should not carry it, and the result is board damage. That is
a real escalation path, and linking it to the board-repair page is following the failure rather than
upselling.

complete.
