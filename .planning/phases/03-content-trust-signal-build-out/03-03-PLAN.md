<objective>
Split category 2 into a short routing hub and five focused child pages, and ship the hub plus the
first two children.

D3-03 accepts the reasoning that панти, матрица, клавиатура, USB/HDMI портове and захранваща букса
are five distinct searches with their own intent, and that one page competing for all five loses all
five. «Екран, клавиатура и портове» therefore becomes a hub at roughly 350 words that passes
authority downward and deliberately targets no competitive keyword of its own; the children link back
up and across to two or three siblings.

This is also where the phase's one genuinely one-way door sits. Every other structural decision in
this phase is either reversible or already walked through by the user in CONTEXT. The five child
filenames are not: CONTEXT hands them to Claude's discretion, and once a slug is published and
indexed, changing it needs a redirect layer and forfeits accumulated signal twice. The first task is
that decision, presented before any file is created.

Purpose: five new search entry points on a site whose existing URLs are frozen, created additively so
nothing is retired and no redirect is introduced.
Output: the hub, two children, and the publish flips that make them reachable.
</objective>
