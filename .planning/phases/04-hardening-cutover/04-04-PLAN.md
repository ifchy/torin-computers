<objective>
Retire the chat-app button from all five slots, point them at the contact page, and make the
terms page tell the truth about what happens to a visitor's data and their device.

Purpose: the button still ships in the tree today even though the requirement behind it was
retired on 2026-09-11 — removing it is Phase 4 work, not a done deal. And CONTACT-06 is not
satisfied by the consent checkbox alone: a cookieless analytics tool avoids the consent trigger,
but transparency is a separate obligation, so the processor, its purpose and the fact that data
leaves the EU/EEA still have to be named.

Output: five CTA slots pointing at `kontakti.html`, roughly a hundred lines of failure history
deleted from the config, two new terms blocks and one corrected, and a phantom cutover blocker
closed as obsolete rather than carried forward.

**Decisions implemented (locked in CONTEXT.md):** D4-07 (no retention — which is what makes the terms text short and what it must accurately state), D4-17 (the chat button is replaced in every CTA slot, corrected from four to five by measurement), and the terms/privacy half of the Claude's-Discretion grant, whose wording is flagged for owner approval before launch as that grant requires.
</objective>
