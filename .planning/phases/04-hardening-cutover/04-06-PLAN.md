<objective>
Take the working hours out of the developer's hands and give the owner a file he can edit,
without making it possible for him to break the site.

Purpose: the hours live in a PHP array today, and a stray quote from a non-technical editor
blanks every page — and he would have no way to tell what he did. They are also written **twice**
(and by one reading three times), so editing one place silently tells a search engine something
different from what the footer says. The banner is the same mechanism with a date gate instead of
a non-empty gate.

Output: a plain-text settings file with a parser that cannot surprise anyone, one source for the
hours across every consumer, a self-expiring closure banner, and a Bulgarian guide that is part
of the deliverable rather than an afterthought.

**Decisions implemented (locked in CONTEXT.md):** D4-23 (a plain-text settings file, never raw PHP, with last-known-good per-key fallback), D4-24 (edited through the control panel's file manager, with a Bulgarian guide as part of the deliverable), D4-25 (the confirmed hours and the unstated weekend closure), D4-26 (the hours stop being duplicated), and D4-27 (an auto-expiring, non-dismissible closure banner that also marks the shop closed in the structured data).
</objective>
