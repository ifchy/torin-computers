<objective>
**This is the phase tracer.** One enquiry, wired end to end through every layer this phase
touches, in production quality, verified by a real submission arriving on a real phone.

Purpose: the contact path does not exist — there is no `<form>` and no handler anywhere in
`src/`. Proving the whole spine on one path before building any of it out is what catches an
architectural dead-end after one commit instead of after five. Every later plan in this phase
expands out from this slice: uploads (04-03), spam guard and the email leg (04-05), analytics
(04-07), and the CTA slots that point at it (04-04).

**What is deliberately not in this slice, and why each is a fill rather than a re-route:**
photo uploads (the `photos[]` control ships in the markup; `torin_collect_uploads()` is added in
04-03 behind the same handler call), the email leg (a second driver registered behind the same
`torin_notify()` interface in 04-05), the HMAC time-trap and rate limiter (the hidden `t` field
ships and is carried through the POST; verification is added in 04-05), and the error re-render
(04-05). None of these change the shape of the pipeline — that is the test of a legitimate
tracer stub.

Output: a working contact page on `public_html/new/`, a Telegram notification on the owner's
device, and the notification interface every later channel plugs into.

**Decisions implemented (locked in CONTEXT.md):** D4-05 (the notification channel is primary), D4-07 (no server-side storage), D4-08 (any one channel succeeding is success), D4-10 (the handler owns its errors — never a blind redirect), the markup half of D4-12 (photos are optional and ship as an optional control), D4-16 (a dedicated contact page), and the no-store half of D4-34 (HTML caching is coupled to the time trap).
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| visitor browser -> `contact-send.php` | Unauthenticated, public, accepts a POST body with attacker-controlled values in every field. The largest single expansion of attack surface in this project's history. |
| `contact-send.php` -> `/home/<user>/torin-secrets.php` | A credential crosses into PHP memory, outside the document root. |
| `notify.php` -> `api.telegram.org` | Outbound HTTPS to a fixed literal host. No inbound webhook, no callback endpoint. |
| `contact-send.php` -> `vendor/phpmailer/` | Not crossed in this plan — the quarantine boundary (A-3) exists from the start so 04-05 does not have to create it. |
