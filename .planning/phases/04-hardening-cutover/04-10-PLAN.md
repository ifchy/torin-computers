<objective>
Perform the swap, and refuse to call it done on anything less than a full automated sweep.

Purpose: this is the moment the rebuilt site becomes the site. The swap itself is cheap and
reversible by construction — server-side renames, no re-upload, a window measured in seconds, and
the old site left on disk as a live safety net. What is not cheap is getting the order wrong, or
declaring success from a homepage glance. A spot-check was explicitly rejected as the go decision
because it would not have caught the redirect defect this project already shipped once.

Output: the redesigned site live at the same URLs, a sweep record with every figure and the command
that produced it, a submitted sitemap, and a written go or no-go.

**Decisions implemented (locked in CONTEXT.md):** D4-28 (a server-side move in both directions, with the old site left on disk as a live safety net), the execution half of D4-31 (the manual deletion pass), the execution half of D4-32 (the full automated sweep with rollback on any failure — a homepage spot-check was explicitly rejected), and the verification of the folded staging-noindex todo against live responses.
</objective>
