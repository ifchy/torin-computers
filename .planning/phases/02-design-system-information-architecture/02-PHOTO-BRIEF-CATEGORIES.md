# Photo Brief #1 — The Six Service Categories

**For:** ТОРИН КОМПЮТЪРС — torin.bg redesign
**Created:** 2026-08-05 (Phase 2)
**Status:** Awaiting owner-supplied photography

---

## Why this exists

The redesign ships with **icons** for the six service categories, because no usable photography exists today. Every category's image slot is deliberately built so an icon can be swapped for a real photo **without any layout change** — drop the file in, and it works.

Real workshop photos are a meaningful upgrade over icons. Competitors use stock imagery; genuine photos of your own bench, your own tools and your own repairs are the one thing they cannot copy. They are the strongest trust signal available to a repair shop.

There is no deadline. Collect these as they naturally occur during work — a phone camera is fine if the guidance below is followed.

---

## Shooting guidance (applies to all shots)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Camera** | A modern phone is fine. Use the main lens, not the ultra-wide — it distorts. |
| **Orientation** | **Landscape (horizontal)**, always. The slots are wider than they are tall. |
| **Resolution** | As large as the phone produces. Do not shrink or crop before sending. |
| **Lighting** | Bright and even. Near a window is ideal. Avoid direct overhead ceiling light — it casts hard shadows into the device. Turn the flash **off**. |
| **Background** | The real bench is good. Clean it first — no coffee cups, no clutter, no personal items, no visible customer names or serial numbers. |
| **Focus** | Tap on the part being worked on. Sharp where it matters. |
| **Include hands** | Photos with hands actively working outperform photos of objects sitting alone. It reads as real work rather than a catalogue. |
| **Avoid** | Faces (unless the person consents), customer data on screens, visible brand logos as the subject, heavy filters, anything staged-looking. |
| **Quantity** | 2–3 shots per category, so there's a choice. |

**Privacy:** never photograph a screen showing a customer's files, email, or personal data. Power the device off or show the BIOS/boot screen instead.

## The six shots

### 1 · Счупвания и механични повреди
*(physical/impact damage, including hinges — see OWNER-QUESTIONS #17)*

**What it should show:** Visible physical damage being addressed — a cracked case, a broken hinge, a bent chassis. The damage should be legible at a glance in a small thumbnail.

**Recommended:** A laptop with a clearly broken hinge or split case corner, held or being disassembled, with a screwdriver or spudger in frame.

**Avoid:** Damage so subtle it's invisible when the image is small.

---

### 2 · Екран, клавиатура и портове

**What it should show:** A component mid-replacement — the moment of swapping, not the finished result.

**Recommended:** A laptop screen panel being lifted away from the lid with the ribbon cable still visible, or a keyboard half-removed from its frame. The "in progress" state communicates skill.

**Avoid:** A closed, finished laptop — that says nothing about the work.

---

### 3 · Оптимизация

**What it should show:** The hardest of the six to photograph, because the work is largely software. Aim for something concrete rather than abstract.

**Recommended options:**
- An SSD being installed in place of a hard drive (a genuinely physical optimization job)
- RAM modules being seated into slots
- A clean, fast boot screen on a laptop on the bench

**Avoid:** A generic screenshot of a speed-test tool — reads as stock imagery.

---

### 4 · Заливане и ремонт на дънни платки
*This is your BGA/chip-level expertise (DIFF-03) — the most technically impressive category. Worth the most effort.*

**What it should show:** Board-level work under magnification. This is what separates you from shops that only swap parts.

**Recommended options:**
- A motherboard under the microscope with a soldering iron or hot-air station in frame
- A close-up of corrosion or liquid damage on a board, before cleaning
- A BGA chip mid-reball, or the reballing stencil in use
- Before/after of a board that was cleaned of corrosion

**Ideal:** the microscope shot. Very few competitors can show one.

**Avoid:** A board just lying on a desk with nothing happening.

---

### 5 · Прегряване и охлаждане
*(renamed from «Смяна на вентилатори» — this category now covers cooling broadly, including профилактика, so lean toward dust/thermal work rather than the fan part alone)*

**What it should show:** Cooling work — ideally the dramatic version.

**Recommended:** A heatsink and fan **thick with dust** being removed, next to the clean replacement. The contrast between filthy and clean is instantly understandable and needs no explanation.

**Also good:** Fresh thermal paste being applied to a CPU/GPU die.

**Avoid:** A new fan still in its packaging.

---

### 6 · Нестандартна техника
*Content for this category is still undefined — see OWNER-QUESTIONS #3. Once the owner clarifies what it covers, this brief should be updated with a specific shot.*

**What it should show:** Whatever the least-expected device you've repaired is. The whole point of this category is "we fix things other shops turn away," so the photo should make someone think *"I didn't know anyone could fix that."*

**Recommended:** The most unusual piece of equipment on the bench — industrial, medical, audio, measurement, vintage. The stranger, the better.

**Avoid:** Another laptop. That defeats the category's purpose entirely.

---

## Bonus: before/after pairs

If any repair naturally produces a striking before/after — a corroded board cleaned up, a dust-choked heatsink made new, a shattered screen replaced — **shoot both states from the same angle**. These are extremely persuasive and are already planned as a v2 feature (GALLERY-01). Same framing, same distance, same lighting; just the two moments.

---

## Sending them over

Send originals — not screenshots, not compressed messenger copies, not resized. WeTransfer, Google Drive, or any large-file method preserves quality. Name them by category if convenient (`kategoriya-4-dunni-platki-01.jpg`), but it isn't essential.
