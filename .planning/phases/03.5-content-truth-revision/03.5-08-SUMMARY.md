# Phase 03.5 Plan 08: Ports-page chip-replacement claim removed — Summary

Removed the last published claim to a discontinued service in the tree — the "the only solution
then is replacement of the chip itself" clause on `src/remont-na-portove.html` — by generalising
the consequence to the board and naming the surviving board-replacement outcome, leaving the
page's mechanism, cause and escalation warning untouched.

## What changed

One file, one paragraph, inside the callout «Скъсаният порт може да стигне до дънната платка».

**Before** (paragraph 2, sentence 1):

> «Резултатът в такива случаи е частична или пълна повреда на южния мост, а единственото решение
> тогава е подмяна на самия чип.»

**After:**

> «Резултатът в такива случаи е частична или пълна повреда на самата дънна платка, а когато
> повредата е стигнала дотам, по-надеждният изход е подмяна на платката, а не ремонт.»

Plus a six-line source comment above it recording why, per D3.5-01 / D3.5-02. The comment is
written entirely in English and quotes none of the strings any gate greps for — `truth-gate.js`
scans comments (its own line 108 says so, deliberately), and both gates in this plan match on
Cyrillic literals.

## Reused wording, attributed

Binding rule 1 was *invent nothing*. Neither half of the replacement is new prose:

| Phrase | Source file | Line | Status of the claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| «самата дънна платка» (as the generalised consequence) | `src/test-laptop.html` | 141 | Established by plan 03.5-06, commit `cda0ffe` — the same chip→board generalisation, same register |
| «по-надеждният изход е подмяна на платката, а не ремонт» | `src/zalivane-technosti.html` | 156 | Published; board replacement is an explicitly surviving service under D3.5-02, and is listed as a `fixes` item at `zalivane-technosti.html:115` |

The connective «а когато повредата е стигнала дотам» is an antecedent adaptation of
zalivane-technosti.html:156's own «Когато повредата е стигнала отвъд тях» — the referent changed
(`тях` = the discrete components there; `дотам` = the board here), the construction did not.

No third style was invented. Precedent 2 (`tokov-udar.html:96`, which pairs a bridge-chip
consequence with «платката обикновено се подменя, а не се ремонтира») was read and matched in
shape; its literal «тези големи корпуси» was not reused because it has no plural antecedent on
this page, and forcing it would have been composition rather than reuse.

## What survived, intact and unmodified

- **The mechanism** — line 102 in full: port pins soldered straight to the board, running directly
  into that chip on many models, and why that direct connection carries the fault inward. Not a
  character changed.
- **The cause** — the line-102 chip mention. The audit placed it as a CAUSE (its own table row:
  `remont-na-portove.html | 102 | chip-noun | cause`), i.e. Class-B and legitimate, exactly like
  the three retained `tokov-udar.html` mentions.
- **The escalation into board-level work** — «Така един разклатен конектор от дребна механична
  повреда се превръща в работа по дънната платка.» True; board-level work is a surviving service.
- **The warning** — «Затова не отлагайте порт, който вече се клати или работи през едно: разликата
  между двата ремонта е голяма, а моментът, в който единият става другият, не се вижда отвън.»
  This is the page's reason for existing. Unchanged.

Nothing was string-deleted. The paragraph is the same length it was.

### The declined-bridge detector: 2 → 1, and why not 0

The audit's headline live measurement for this page was `served-declined-bridge=2`. Source now
returns **1**. The remaining hit is the line-102 CAUSE, deliberately retained — the objective's
success criteria require the cause and mechanism to survive *intact*, and the Class-A/Class-B rule
that keeps three such mentions on `tokov-udar.html` (which is MET at a count of 3) keeps this one.

**A nonzero count on this detector is therefore not a failure signal, and a future re-audit must
not read it as one.** If the phase owner later wants this page to reach 0, the established
precedent already exists and is cheap: plan 03.5-06 rewrote the near-identical sentence on
`mehanichni-problemi.html:111` to «влизат директно в **управляващия чип на платката**». Applying
that same swap to line 102 here would take the count to 0 without losing the mechanism. It was
**not** done in this plan because it is outside the objective's stated scope, which names only the
cure as false and requires the cause to survive intact. Flagging it as an owner decision, not a
defect.
