<objective>
Rewrite category 4 — «Заливане и ремонт на дънни платки» — to the service the shop still offers.

This is the single hardest page in the phase and the one where careless subtraction does the most
damage. Of its four `fixes` entries, three are chip-level and go, leaving one. Its fifth FAQ answers
«други сервизи ми отказаха» entirely on the strength of the infrared station, so its premise is
gone. Its evidence strip has a chip-level caption. Its «кога ремонтът не си струва» paragraph turns
on liquid reaching under the chips. Strip all of that and what remains is a page about a category
the owner has explicitly kept as one of the six headline services — with a hole where its substance
used to be.

D3.5-02 is the instruction that makes this tractable: the category is **re-scoped, not removed**. It
now covers liquid cleaning, corrosion removal, **non-BGA** component-level soldering (capacitors,
mosfets, fuses, connectors) and **board replacement** where the case calls for it. That re-scope is
the owner's own words, recorded in OWNER-QUESTIONS #31 — it is a source, not an invention, and it is
what the rewritten page is built from.

Why `requirements: [DIFF-04]`: REQUIREMENTS.md records DIFF-04 as existing because DIFF-02 and
DIFF-03 retired. Removing DIFF-03's claims from the page that carried the most of them is part of
that requirement's delivery.

Purpose: keep a headline category honest and complete at the same time.
Output: one rewritten service page, measured in Cyrillic tokens, with every sentence traceable.
</objective>
