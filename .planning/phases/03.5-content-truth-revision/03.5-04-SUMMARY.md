### THE RULING — paste on the main packages (recorded once, applied to both pages)

> **`Смяна на термопастата на процесора и основните чипсети` KEEPS its chip-package noun on both
> pages.**

Basis:

1. The Class-B rule exists to stop a chip noun smuggling a **withdrawn service claim** back onto a
   page. Thermal paste is applied to the *outside* of a chip package. D3.5-02 withdraws work on the
   chip's solder interface; it does not withdraw work on top of the package. No customer reads this
   line as an offer of chip-level repair.
2. Rewording it would make it **less** true. The paste genuinely goes on the processor and the main
   packages; naming a different component set to dodge a noun would be writing a different claim,
   and D3.5-08 forbids that more strongly than the Class-B rule asks.
3. **Stated honestly: this is a fourth category, not one of the three the gate names.** The gate's
   rule permits SYMPTOM, DIAGNOSTIC OBSERVATION and CAUSE. This is none of those — it is a
   **LOCATION inside a description of a surviving service**. It is offered as an extension of the
   rule with its reasoning on the record, not smuggled in under an existing heading. **03.5-07
   should confirm or overturn it deliberately.** If overturned, both pages change together and the
   ruling is amended here, in one place.
