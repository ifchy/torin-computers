<objective>
Correct the three pages whose *premise* was chip-level repair rather than a sentence of it, and
record honestly every gap the correction opens.

These three are different from the pure-removal pages. `about.html` sells the shop on being the
place that repairs at chip level — it is in the description, the intro, the company history, a
dedicated equipment paragraph and an FAQ answer, and the history also states in the present tense
that the shop supplies batteries. `tokov-udar.html` opens and closes on repairing surge damage at
chip level. `problem-stari.html` is the category-6 page — the one requirement DIFF-04 is being
promoted to carry — and it describes the work that *survives* using the vocabulary that was
withdrawn.

That last distinction is the one this plan turns on. The phrase «на ниво чип» and the phrase «на
ниво компонент» describe different work. The first is the BGA process the shop no longer performs.
The second — repairing power circuits and replacing individual failed parts on a board — is exactly
what D3.5-02 keeps. `problem-stari.html` already describes the second and labels it with the first.
That is a vocabulary correction, not a deletion, and getting it right is what stops this phase from
accidentally deleting the shop's surviving capability along with the withdrawn one.

`about.html` gets no such rescue. Its equipment paragraph describes equipment used for a service
that is gone, and nothing replaces it.

Why the three requirement IDs: DIFF-04 and CONTENT-01 both attach to category 6, which this plan's
third page is. SEO-05 attaches because two of these pages still link to the retired battery page.

Purpose: correct the pages whose whole argument changed, and file the gaps rather than fill them.
Output: three corrected pages and new, numbered owner questions.
</objective>
